Judges, not colleagues. Speak slowly. Pause on the grade.

1. **Home (0:00–0:25)** — The problem: people shield and think they disappeared. The doors stay public. Thesis on screen: “See what this action still reveals.”
2. **Protocol (0:25–0:50)** — Hidden vs visible for shield, send, unshield. Say out loud: a shield is never quiet. No viewing keys.
3. **Vault, disconnected (0:50–1:10)** — Look back empty. Connect Ready. Stay on mainnet.
4. **Look back (1:10–1:35)** — Public edges for this address. Point at source (`live` / `fixture`). Pause on any rapid in-and-out row.
5. **Look ahead (1:35–2:10)** — Type an amount that matches a recent deposit. Unshield. Pause on **LOUD**. Apply a quieter path (change amount or wait).
6. **Close (2:10–2:30)** — You still sign in Ready. Lens does not hold a key and does not hide the door.

Do not show Cairo. Do not claim an anonymity-set size. Do not invent a pool-wide statistic.
